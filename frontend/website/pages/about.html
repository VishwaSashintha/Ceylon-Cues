OUR STORY & HERITAGE

Precision. Performance. Passion.

Ceylon Cues was founded in Sri Lanka with a single mission: to elevate the island’s billiards, snooker, and pool community with authentic, tournament-grade cues, chalk, and precision table equipment.

The Ceylon Cues Standard

For decades, Sri Lankan cue sports enthusiasts faced limited access to genuine equipment, often relying on warped house cues or inconsistent tips. Ceylon Cues bridges that gap through direct manufacturing partnerships and strict quality verification.

Every single cue in our warehouse undergoes a comprehensive 4-point inspection: shaft straightness roll testing, ferrule alignment, tip density testing, and joint tolerance checks before it reaches your hands.
