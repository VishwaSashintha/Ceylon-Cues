Customer Protection & Store Policies

Our commitments regarding shipping, returns, warranty, and customer data privacy.

1. Shipping & Island-wide Delivery

Ceylon Cues ships island-wide across all 25 districts in Sri Lanka via professional tracked courier partners. Orders within the Western Province (Colombo, Gampaha, Kalutara) are generally delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Orders to all other districts are delivered within 2 to 4 business days.

Free Delivery: Orders exceeding our free delivery threshold (LKR 15,000.00) qualify for complimentary island-wide courier shipping. Customers may also elect "Collect from Shop (Free)" at checkout to pick up items immediately at our Colombo 03 showroom.

2. 14-Day Return & Replacement Policy

If your order arrives damaged or defective, or if a cue exhibits warping out of the box, we will gladly replace it or issue a full refund within 14 days of receipt.

Items must be returned in their original packaging with all included accessories. Chalk or tip shaping tools that have been heavily used or modified are excluded from change-of-mind returns.

3. Cue Sports Warranty

All Standard and Premium tier cues come with a 6-month structural warranty covering joint thread defects, ferrule bonding failures, and butt delamination under standard playing conditions. Warranty does not cover normal tip wear or damage resulting from improper moisture exposure.

4. Privacy & Data Erasure (GDPR / Right to be Forgotten)

Ceylon Cues respects your privacy. We use your contact number solely for WhatsApp/SMS delivery updates and tax invoicing. We never share your personal data with third-party marketers. Customers may request full personal data anonymization at any time through our customer support portal, while preserving tax invoice continuity as required by Sri Lankan financial regulations.

5. Payment Verification & Bank Transfers

For direct bank transfers, orders remain in "Awaiting Verification" until our finance team confirms that the deposit matches the bank statement with a readable, non-reused transfer slip. Confirmed payments are processed and dispatched on the same business day.
