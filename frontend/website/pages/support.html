Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take across Sri Lanka?

Deliveries within the Western Province (Colombo, Gampaha, Kalutara) take 24 to 48 hours. Island-wide courier deliveries to all other districts typically take 2 to 4 business days with end-to-end waybill tracking.

What payment methods are supported?

We accept Direct Bank Transfer (Commercial Bank of Ceylon), Visa / Mastercard, KOKO (3 installments), Mintpay, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible standard orders.

Can I test cues in person before buying?

Yes! You are welcome to visit our retail showroom at No. 45 Galle Road, Colombo 03, where our staff will allow you to test cue weight and balance on our demonstration table.
